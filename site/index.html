Spindle

An Android record player for rainy nights, with Cantonese gaohu, Satie on guitar, Chopin after midnight and ragtime for the last ferry. Built with Kotlin, Jetpack Compose and Media3. Every track is public domain or Creative Commons.

Download the APK Source on GitHub

The APK installs on Android 8.0 or newer (allow installs from your browser when asked). Or listen right here: this page plays the same catalog the app uses.

What the app does

Plays in the background

A Media3 MediaSessionService keeps music going with the screen off, with notification, lock-screen and headphone controls, and handles audio focus.

A turntable on the album page

The cover spins as a record while its album plays and stops where it is when you pause.

Favorites, offline

Hearted albums are stored in Room and show up even without a connection.

One catalog, three ways

A Ktor server, this page and the app all read the same catalog through shared Kotlin models.

Spindle is a portfolio project by Hao (Miles) Zhang, rebuilt from a LaiOffer course app. Album groupings are mine; recordings and cover art belong to their creators and are credited on each album.
